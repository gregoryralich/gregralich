# Household Haz Com — business-plan ideas
Recorded October 29, 2026.

## Storage is a core service
The inventory creates an opportunity to improve storage before disposal. Prioritize damaged containers for prompt assessment regardless of category; consider shelf stability, incompatible products, flood exposure, ignition sources, children/pets, and slips/trips. Give compatible secondary-containment guidance for suitable identified materials. Unknown leaks, corroded containers, reactive products, and damaged batteries require specialist routing.

## Battery experience
Practical and friendly battery guidance is a central proposed differentiator beyond conventional disposal wizards: identify chemistry and condition, explain interim storage, verify specific-site acceptance, and close the loop. Validate competitor differentiation in future research.
