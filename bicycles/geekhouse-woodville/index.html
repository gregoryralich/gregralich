Geekhouse Woodville

My touring bike

Built in Boston for a cross-country ride in 2010. Yellow first, green later, and still rolling.

Score!

I did my college "internship" at Geekhouse Bikes working with Marty Walsh. I worked for 6 months, got course credits, and a bike ready to ride cross-country. Very grateful to Marty for making this possible for young Greg.

The one-piece bar-stem was built by Ian of Icarus Frames. He had made his bike most of the way across the country a couple of years before and was a major source of inspiration and support.

04 · In print

Embrocation Cycling Journal.

I wrote a story about this bicycle for Embrocation Cycling Journal. I had crashed the bike really bad, getting doored by a car in Brighton and experienced some PTSD preventing me from getting back on! A gripping tale that sold 10s of copies.

05 · Stolen & returned

Back to me.

This bike was stolen—and eventually returned to me. After I got it back, I rebuilt it and had it powder coated green.

06 · After the return

A second life, in green.

The green powder coat came only after the bike was returned and rebuilt. It has had a flat-bar-and-basket setup, and today mixes those histories with drop bars and an older Shimano 105 group.
