My touring bike

Photographs from before and during my 2010 cross-country trip are below. More about the bike and its build coming soon.

The story.

I did my college "internship" or working practicum at Geekhouse Bikes working with Marty Walsh. I worked for 6 months, got course credits, and a bike ready to ride cross-country. Very grateful to Marty for making this possible for young Greg.

The build.

The bike has seen multiple distinct builds - the original cross country setup: yellow with an Ultegra triple, after it was stolen and returned I repainted it green and set it up with flat bars and basket, today it is a mix of each history with drop bars and a nice older 105 group. The only originaly part is the Chris King headset.
