Firefly ATB

Built for long distances on mountain bike trails.

A 2026 Firefly ATB, informed by sixteen years of riding my Geekhouse Woodville.

The story.

I saw Tyler riding his ATB at D2R2 and thought, “This is the bike people will be riding in 2030. This is my chance to get ahead of the game.”

The starting point was sixteen years on my Geekhouse Woodville, the bike I rode across the country. Touring with that bike taught me a lot about carrying gear. The Firefly and the Woodville go hand in hand.

This is my first bike with thru-axles, a 1× drivetrain, carbon wheels, a UDH, electronic shifting, or hydraulic brakes. A lot of firsts in one bike. It’s my Firefly dream machine.

The build.

  • Frame: 2026 Firefly ATB, titanium
  • Fork: Firefly ATB titanium
  • Stem, headset, and spacers: Firefly
  • Seatpost: Dropper
  • Drivetrain: SRAM Force AXS electronic shifting, 1×, 10–52 cassette
  • Wheels: Carbon, thru-axle
  • Derailleur hanger: UDH
  • Brakes: Hydraulic
  • Racks: Front and rear titanium racks made by Tyler

Firefly’s ATB page has more on the frame and its design.

Carrying gear.

I went from a trailer to micro panniers, skipping frame bags: Rockgeist up front and Revelate in the back. Tyler was kind enough to build me front and rear titanium racks for the setup.

Fully loaded, I can carry the bike for up to two miles at a time.
