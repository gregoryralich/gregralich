Firefly ATB

Built just for me.

A 2026 Firefly ATB, informed by sixteen years of riding my Geekhouse Woodville.

The story.

I saw Tyler riding his new ATB at D2R2 in 2024 and thought, “This is the bike people will be riding in 2040. This could be my chance to get ahead of the game.”

The starting point was sixteen years on my Geekhouse Woodville, the bike I rode across the country. Touring with that bike taught me a lot about carrying gear. The Firefly and the Woodville go hand in hand. I logged a lot of miles on my Geekhouse alongside the Firefly Bicycles team. Patiently waiting. Studying their trajectory. Plotting my perfect moment to pounce. In 2025 the world was looking pretty bleak and I thought... If the world ends and I never got a Firefly - what was the whole point?

This is my first bike with thru-axles, a 1× drivetrain, carbon wheels, a UDH, electronic shifting, or hydraulic brakes. A lot of firsts in one bike. It’s my Firefly dream machine. Many of these technologies became normal and tested over the last decade. I wonder what new tech will come out in 10-20 years?

The build.

This is honestly for my own record keeping purposes as much as anything else. Firefly gives you a cute little USB with your bike with photos and deets. I share them with you now:

  • Frame: 2026 Firefly ATB, titanium
  • Fork: Firefly ATB titanium with cargo mounts
  • Stem: Firefly titanium, 90mm, −6°
  • Headset, top cap, and spacers: Firefly titanium
  • Handlebars: Zipp SL-70 XPLR, 42cm
  • Bar tape: Silca Nastro Cuscino
  • Brake and shift controls: SRAM Force AXS
  • Rear derailleur: SRAM GX Eagle Transmission, UDH interface
  • Crankset: White Industries A30, 170mm, black
  • Chainring: White Industries MR30 TSR 1×, 38t, 0mm offset, black
  • Bottom bracket: White Industries BSA30, black
  • Cassette: SRAM XS-1295 Eagle, 10–52
  • Chain: SRAM X0 Eagle Transmission
  • Battery: SRAM AXS
  • Brakes: SRAM Force AXS hydraulic discs with internal hose routing
  • Rotors: SRAM Paceline X, 180mm front / 160mm rear
  • Wheels: Reserve 28XC carbon rims with DT Swiss 350 hubs, thru-axle
  • Tires: Rene Herse Fleecer Ridge, Endurance casing
  • Tubes: Rene Herse TPU
  • Seatpost: RockShox Reverb AXS, 125mm travel, 30.9mm diameter
  • Saddle: Specialized Romin Evo Comp with Mimic, 143mm
  • Bottle cages: Three King Cage titanium cages
  • Handlebar bag: Routewerks Large, black, with 31.8mm mount, Quad Lock integrated tech mount, and handlebar stub
  • Racks: Front and rear titanium racks made by Tyler

Carrying gear.

I went from a backpack to a trailer to micro panniers. I skipped the frame bag generation of bikepacking. Rockgeist up front and Revelate in the back. I like 'em both. Defunct-company-but-awesome-bag: RouteWorks on the handlebars. Tyler was kind enough to build me front and rear titanium racks for the setup. Fully loaded, I can still carry the bike well which is nice.
