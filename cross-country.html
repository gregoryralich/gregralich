Uh Oh! In my infinite time spent blogging for a million different outlets I have fallen behind.  Posts here have been few and far between but, like always, it’s all for a good cause!  Bianca and I are officially done with classes and we’re both eagerly awaiting May 23rd when we get to walk across the stage and receive our $80,000 pieces of paper saying that we accomplished something over the last four years

My bike is almost done and after a small setback in the paint department should be completed by this time next week!  While I’ve had a blast walking and using what the locals refer to as the “T” I’m very ready to ride again.  My collarbone is feeling great! I think it is actually stronger than it was before but we’ll see.. Today I carried a backpack ALL day. Impressive right?

We’ll be back in the swing of things soon enough.  I know I’ll be all over New England getting our my ready for the big departure around July 1st- so shoot me an e-mail if you want me to come visit you, I’m feeling flabby and need to start logging some miles as soon as I tighten that saddle down.

Expect a new update e-mail soon.. if you aren’t on the e-mail list just drop me a line at gregralich@gmail.com and I’ll see what I can do.

-Gregory
