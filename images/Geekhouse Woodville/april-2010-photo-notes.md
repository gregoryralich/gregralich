# Geekhouse Woodville — April 2010 close-ups

Photographs by Tim Ralich, Greg Ralich's younger brother, taken in April 2010 after the bike was built and before Greg's cross-country trip.

The one-piece bar-stem combination was built by Ian of Icarus Frames: a steel stem fillet-brazed to a steel handlebar, with Brooks leather bar tape and Cane Creek brake levers.
