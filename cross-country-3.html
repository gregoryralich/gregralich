Penn's Silvae

Affter a 30 mile mad dash through North Philadelphia yesterday (to get to a bike shop before it closed) we have arrived at our destination in Malvern, PA where we will be taking a few days off to re-organize, send some stuff home, hot tub, and spend time with my bestest of buds Mike. It was such a treat to be welcomed last night by Mike and his family, our friend Nicki, and of course Mike’s dog Vince who I haven’t seen for almost 2 years! We have successfully completed the first 400 miles of our trip and we couldn’t have asked for a better first leg of our journey. We’re spending a few days in Malvern while our bikes get a spa treatment in Philly (more on that later), to map out the next 500-1,000 miles, and of course.. upload all the pictures we haven’t been able to put up from the road. It’s gonna to be a bit of a post overload, especially since these posts all appear on our Facebook pages too, but I promise we’ve got some gems coming your way so stay tuned.

-Gregory

Apparently this “arch” marks the spot of the first McDonald’s ever! I know what you’re thinking and I thought it too- isn’t McDonald’s logo- two, golden arches? Well, apparently when they were founded in 1994 they started with only one silver arch. Cool right? and really makes you think..

Kansas City Here I Come/Am

After bonking out pretty hard yesterday in the 100 degree heat we finally made it to Kansas City where we are staying with my (Greg’s) godmother. We packed on the protein last night with some pretty epic BBQ and got a great introduction to a state that is basically the opposite of Massachusetts. When we stayed on the Katy Trail in Missouri we heard some pretty wild things about Kansas but my Aunt and two cousins sorted the truths from the half truths regarding rattlesnakes and tornadoes. So now, we are taking the day off to watch our quad muscles grow before our eyes and run some errands. With Kansas checked off on our list that puts 11 states down and only 4 to go! Mar Mar I’ll be home before you know it!
