2009–2011 · Boston · Bicycle fabrication

Geekhouse Bikes.

From 2009 to 2011, I worked in an office management and entry-level marketing role at Geekhouse Bikes. We sold about 100 bicycles a year, all custom designed and fabricated in Boston—including machining, welding, and in-house powder coating. I really enjoyed doing the newsletters, Flickr, early-Instagram and working on the CX Team and going to NAHBS.
Dan St. Germain was doing very creative things online then and it was cool to watch over his shoulder. I also got to meet Aaron Panone and the Fringe community through my time at Geekhouse.

Geekhouse is no longer around as a business, but The Radavist's Geekhouse archive documented our glory days best.

2010 · Boston to San Francisco · 4,000+ miles

Cross-Country for Autism.

In 2010, I rode more than 4,000 miles with my friend Bianca, from Boston to San Francisco—against the wind. We raised more than $5,000 for the Autism Society of America, inspired by my mom's 30 years of work in pediatric nursing.
