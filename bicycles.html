Goma · Democratic Republic of Congo

Tumaini Cycling Academy

Getting kids on bikes and in school. Tumaini Cycling Academy supports young people in Goma through bicycles, education, mentorship, and a community invested in their future.

I’m working with cyclist and coach Joel Kyaviro to support the academy through fundraising, outreach, and its website. We’re raising money for bicycles, equipment, and a bike shop to help more young riders get started.

2009–2011 · Boston · Bicycle fabrication

Geekhouse Bikes.

From 2009 to 2011, I worked in an office management and entry-level marketing role at Geekhouse Bikes. We sold about 100 bicycles a year, all custom designed and fabricated in Boston — including machining, welding, and in-house powder coating. I really enjoyed doing the newsletters, Flickr, early-Instagram and working on the CX Team and going to NAHBS.
Dan St. Germain was doing very creative things online then and it was cool to watch over his shoulder. I also got to meet Aaron Panone and the Fringe community through my time at Geekhouse.

Geekhouse is no longer around as a business, but The Radavist's Geekhouse archive documented our glory days best.

2010 · Boston to San Francisco · 4,000+ miles

Cross-Country for Autism.

In 2010, I rode more than 4,000 miles with my friend Bianca, from Boston to San Francisco — against the wind. We raised more than $5,000 for the Autism Society of America, inspired by my mom's 30 years of work in pediatric nursing.

February 2018 · North American Handmade Bicycle Show

NAHBS with Hot Tubes Paint.

In February 2018, I joined Jordan Low in the Hot Tubes Paint booth at NAHBS. Jordan and I go way back, and I was honored to help curate the booth.

The booth featured a bike built by Luke of Concinnity Cycle Works. Firefly typically doesn’t showcase at bike shows, so we were proud to showcase a custom-painted Firefly road bike.

I made laser-cut signage and a map of the show space highlighting bikes in other booths that had been painted by Hot Tubes.

My Seven Tsunami was repainted to show how a paint job could breathe new life into a 20-year-old bike. The only bike with canti studs at the show!

2015 · Cuppow × Geekhouse · Cyclocross

Cuppow Geekhouse CX Team.

At Cuppow, we sponsored the Geekhouse cyclocross team as a way to support another small business and the cycling community. It was an alternative to traditional advertising: putting our support behind people, a sport, and a way of life we believed in.

In May 2015, The Spoken interviewed me as we headed toward our fourth season of sponsorship. We talked about Adria Klora’s bold kit designs, the unmistakable Team Mudville bikes, and our growing support for elite New England riders through a partnership with B2C2.
